Frequently asked questions

Answers before you enquire.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about samples, custom sizes, bulk orders and project support.

Still curious? Ask us →
How do I request a product quote or specification sheet?

Fill in the enquiry form above with the product, application, estimated quantity and destination. We use those details to prepare a SKU-specific specification and quote.

Can I order samples or swatches before a bulk order?

Yes. Choose “Sample request” in the enquiry type and tell us which fibre, weave or format you want to evaluate. Sample availability depends on the product.

Do you offer custom sizes, colours or private labelling?

Custom dimensions and finishes are discussed per product. Share the target size, quantity and any branding needs so we can confirm what is practical.

Do you supply wholesale and export quantities?

Yes. Bulk and export enquiries are welcome, including bales, rolls and palletised products. Include your destination port and preferred timeline in the form.

Can you help with geo-textile and erosion-control projects?

Select “Geo-textile project” and share the slope, soil, exposure and area details. We will help match mesh, logs or mats to the site conditions.

How long does it take to get a reply?

Our trade desk answers enquiries Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST. Complete details help us respond faster.
